What to expect.

Live sessions

Small groups meet by video with an experienced professional, practice the skill during the session and get direct feedback.

Workshops

Longer sessions held in person on campuses and in regional cities, with time to work through real problems in small groups.

Applied assignments

Each session ends with a task to complete that week, such as a speech, a meeting agenda or an outreach plan.

A national cohort

Students from colleges across the country and from every viewpoint work through the material together.

Earn a Society certificate.

Participants who complete the program requirements earn a Leadership Society certificate they can list on their résumé.

Common questions.

Who can apply?
Students enrolled at a college or university, in any major. Educators can write to us to advise a student organization or bring a session to a class.
How do I get involved?
Complete the application by Monday, December 28, 2026, at 11:59 PM CST. After review, we contact applicants for an interview, online or in person.
Does it cost anything?
No. The Leadership Society is free for every participant. There are no application fees, program fees or dues.
Is the Leadership Society partisan?
No. The Leadership Society is nonpartisan. We teach the practical skills of leadership, including speaking, organizing, advocacy and professional conduct, to prepare students for capable professional leadership in any field.
Are there scholarships?
Scholarships are available to select students. More on the Scholarships page.
