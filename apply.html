Apply to the Society.

Tell us who you are and what you want to lead. Applications for the 2027 cohort close Monday, December 28, 2026, at 11:59 PM CST.

What happens next

Our team reviews each application and contacts applicants for an interview, online or in person.

Students at any college or university may apply.

Questions about the application: recruitment@leadershipsocietyamerica.org

The Leadership Society trains driven college students in the practical skills of leadership: speaking / conveying a message to large groups, organizing people, building coalitions and turning ideas into results. Our mission is to identify students with the character and work ethic to lead, and to give them the training, network and opportunities to do it well.

Who should apply. We encourage every student who meets our criteria to apply. Every major, professional field and classification is welcome, from first-year undergraduates to graduate students. Students of any citizenship and immigration status are welcome to apply so long as you are currently enrolled at a college or university.

Eligibility Requirements: Applicants must be 18 years of age or older and have a cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher.

This is a selective, cohort-based program. Admitted students train alongside a small group of peers through a structured series of sessions that prepare them for leadership roles on campus and beyond. Cohort members may become eligible for scholarships and for internship opportunities in the Houston area matched to their skills, interests and goals.

Deadline: Monday, December 28, 2026, at 11:59 PM CST.

Selected applicants will be invited to an orientation meeting where the program is explained in full.

For any questions about the Leadership Society or your application, reach out to recruitment@leadershipsocietyamerica.org. Every application will be reviewed.

You are welcome to share this application and encourage others to apply.

Applicants invited to interview, attend an information session, or join the program will have access to information about how Leadership Society operates. By accepting an invitation, you agree to the following:

1. CONFIDENTIALITY. Do not share, post, record, or forward what you learn through Leadership Society with anyone outside it unless you are asked to. This includes our training, materials, strategies, and information about other applicants and participants.

2. COMPETING PROGRAMS. Do not share it with any other organization, group, or individual that offers similar programs or services, such as leadership development, advocacy training, or campaign training.
