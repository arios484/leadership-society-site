Lead your student organization.

Sessions and support for officers and members who lead and grow the organizations they already belong to.

How it works.

For officers and members of any registered student organization.

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your organization and your role in it.

  2. Bring your team

    Fellow officers and members can apply alongside you.

  3. Learn together

    Sessions on officer roles, budgets, recruitment and succession.

  4. Grow the organization

    Event formats and follow-up support to apply what you learned.

Support from the Society.

Officer toolkit

Templates for agendas, budgets, recruitment plans and handover notes.

Speaker and event kits

Proven formats for a speaker night, a debate, a panel or a workshop.

Session visits

Online or in-person sessions for your members, led by experienced professionals.

Advise a student organization, or bring a session to class.

Faculty and staff can request a session for a class or student group, support the organizations they advise, or use the event kits on their own. Write to info@leadershipsocietyamerica.org.

Officers and members can apply together for the 2027 cohort.
