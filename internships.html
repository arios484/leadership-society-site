From cohort to your first job.

We match students in the program with internships and support them through the first weeks of the role.

Placements with credible organizations.

The Society places students in the program with internships in public offices, nonprofits and businesses.

  1. Join the program

    Apply to the Leadership Society for the 2027 cohort.

  2. Prepare

    Complete the Foundations sessions and “Starting an internship.”

  3. Match

    We find a placement that fits your interests and schedule.

  4. Start well

    Check-ins during your first weeks on the job.

Our growing list of partner offices will soon be announced here.

Résumé review

Mock interviews and feedback, online.

Mentoring

Conversations with people working in the fields you are aiming for.
